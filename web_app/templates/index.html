    Key Methodological Findings:

    • Handling Code-Mixing: Sub-word and character-level embeddings significantly outperform standard dictionary lookups by gracefully handling spelling variations like "dardddd" and "novoo".
    • Language Discrepancy: English achieves consistent 88-98% F1 across models, while Kanglish and Hinglish show strong gains when incorporating language-aware affixes and Indic tokenization.
    • Inference Latency: The CRF baseline operates in < 5ms on CPU, making it ideal for edge telemedicine stations, while IndicBERT and XLM-RoBERTa deliver deep semantic contextualization.
